Privacy Policy — Lush & Living Finds

Last updated: September 1, 2026

Lush & Living Finds is a lifestyle content brand featuring curated home, kitchen, beauty, hair care, and self-care products. Its digital content publishing and automation systems are managed through Avenro Digital.

This Privacy Policy explains how Lush & Living Finds uses digital tools, automation, third-party services, and official platform APIs, including the Pinterest API, to manage and publish content.

Pinterest API Usage

Lush & Living Finds may use the official Pinterest API to manage, organize, schedule, and publish content to its own Pinterest business account.

Pinterest authentication and authorization are handled through Pinterest's official systems.

We do not collect or store Pinterest passwords.

Information We Collect

Our content publishing application is primarily an internal automation and content management system.

We do not intentionally collect personal information from Pinterest users through this application.

Certain technical information may be processed by third-party platforms when necessary for the operation of their services.

How Information Is Used

Information processed through our systems may be used to:

  • manage our own social media content;
  • organize and publish approved posts;
  • manage Pinterest boards and Pins;
  • operate automated content publishing workflows;
  • monitor workflow performance; and
  • troubleshoot technical issues.

Third-Party Services

Our systems may use third-party services including:

  • Pinterest
  • Amazon
  • automation platforms
  • cloud storage providers
  • artificial intelligence services
  • social media platforms

These third-party services may process information according to their own privacy policies and terms.

Affiliate Disclosure

Lush & Living Finds participates in affiliate programs, including the Amazon Associates Program.

As an Amazon Associate, we earn from qualifying purchases.

Some content may contain affiliate links. We may receive a commission when a qualifying purchase is made through one of these links.

Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect credentials and information used by our internal publishing systems.

API keys, authentication tokens, passwords, and other private credentials are not intentionally displayed publicly on this website.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when our services, integrations, or legal requirements change.

Updates will be published on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.

Contact

For questions regarding this Privacy Policy, Avenro Digital, or Lush & Living Finds, please contact us through the Contact section of the Avenro Digital website.
